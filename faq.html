Quick answers

Questions worth clarifying early.

These answers help prepare a useful enquiry. Final product suitability, price, availability and work scope depend on the confirmed project specification.

How do I request a gabion box quotation?
Share the required dimensions, size-wise quantities, mesh opening/type, wire diameter, coating requirements, accessories and delivery location. Attach the BOQ or project specification if available.
Can you quote supply-only and supply with installation?
Please state which scope you need. Product supply and on-site installation can be quoted separately where feasible. The written offer should clarify stone, geotextile, labour, equipment, transport, unloading and other inclusions.
What determines gabion box price?
Price depends on dimensions, mesh and wire specification, coating, accessories, quantity, packing, freight, destination, unloading arrangements and taxes or other commercial terms. A rate without its inclusions may not be comparable.
Do you supply GI and PVC-coated gabion boxes?
Product availability and the exact coating must be confirmed for each enquiry. For a technical requirement, share the specified product data, drawings or approved-make requirement. Do not assume an unlisted coating or standard is available.
Can I request a quote without final dimensions?
Yes. Share the intended application, approximate size or area, location and any preliminary drawings. The quote may need to wait until the product specification and quantities are confirmed.
Do you deliver across India?
The website is designed to accept India-wide enquiries. Actual delivery capability, freight and feasibility must be confirmed for the specific product, quantity and destination; this is not a promise of universal delivery coverage.
Are installation drawings and engineering design included?
Not by default. Design, engineering review, drawings, supervision and approvals must be explicitly included in the agreed scope. Gabion and retaining structures should follow the project design prepared or approved by the responsible professional.
How should I plan a bulk order?
Provide the RFQ or BOQ, size-wise quantity schedule, technical specification, required delivery window, destination, unloading capacity and any inspection or documentation requirements.
Do you have completed projects displayed on this website?
The current gallery contains illustrative assets and placeholders, not verified company case studies. Actual project photographs and details should be added only after the owner confirms them and has permission to publish.
Does the website send my enquiry automatically?
The site does not have a server-side form backend. When a verified WhatsApp number is configured, submitting the form opens a WhatsApp message for your review and manual sending. Without that setup, the website prepares a message for copying and states that it has not been sent.
Need a specific answer? Include your drawings, dimensions and location in the enquiry so the response can address your actual scope.
