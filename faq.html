FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers that help you prepare a useful enquiry. Final product suitability, price, availability and work scope depend on the confirmed project specification.

Gabion products

What is a gabion box?

A gabion box is a wire-mesh container filled with stone and used for retaining walls, bank protection and erosion control. See gabion boxes.

What is the difference between GI and PVC-coated gabion boxes?

GI boxes use zinc-coated wire; PVC-coated boxes have an additional PVC layer over zinc-coated wire. The choice is set by the project specification. See GI gabion boxes and PVC-coated gabion boxes.

What is a gabion mattress?

A shallow, wide mesh unit filled with stone and used as lining for channel beds, banks and slopes. See gabion mattress.

How do I request a gabion box quotation?

Send dimensions, quantity per size, mesh, wire, coating and delivery location through the quotation form.

What determines gabion box price?

Dimensions, mesh and wire specification, coating, accessories, quantity, packing, freight, destination, unloading and commercial terms. A rate without its inclusions cannot be compared.

Do you publish price lists?

No. Prices are project-specific and are quoted per enquiry.

Labour and construction

Do you supply gabion labour only?

Yes, labour-only enquiries are welcome. State which materials, tools and facilities you provide. See gabion labour supplier.

What affects the gabion labour rate?

Quantity and unit of measurement, stone size, access, headcount, duration, safety needs and who provides tools and accommodation.

Do you build gabion walls to my design?

Yes. Work follows your drawings and specification. Engineering design is not included unless agreed in writing. See gabion wall construction.

Which other civil works do you take up?

Stone pitching, UCR stone wall, soling, PCC and trimix flooring, plus labour supply. See all services.

Is stone included in the quotation?

That depends on the scope. The written quotation states who supplies stone, geotextile, equipment and transport.

Ordering and locations

Can I enquire without final dimensions?

Yes. Share the application, approximate size or area, and location. A final quotation needs confirmed quantities.

How should I plan a bulk order?

Prepare a size-wise schedule, specification, delivery window, destination and unloading details. See bulk orders.

Do you serve Pune and Maharashtra?

Enquiries from Pune, other parts of Maharashtra and other states are welcome. Availability and delivery are confirmed for each site.

Do you deliver across India?

Delivery capability, freight and feasibility are confirmed for the product, quantity and destination; no universal delivery is promised.
