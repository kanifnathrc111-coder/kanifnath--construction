Gabion box installation labour

Gabion installation involves unfolding and shaping baskets, wiring them together, positioning, filling with stone, lacing and closing. You can enquire for labour-only work, or for installation together with materials. State clearly what you will provide.

Gabion wall labour contractor scope

  • Assembly of gabion boxes and mattresses
  • Placing, aligning and lacing units
  • Stone filling and packing
  • Closing lids and finishing the wall face
  • Support for related work such as backfill and stone pitching, as agreed

Labour-only or supply with labour?

Labour-only means you supply gabion units and stone. Supply with labour means we quote the boxes (gabion box supply) and the installation together. The written quotation should clearly list tools, transport, supervision, accommodation and site facilities and who provides them.

What we need for a labour quotation

  1. ScopeWhich tasks: assembly, placing, filling, lacing, finishing.
  2. QuantityNumber of boxes, wall length and height, mattress area or BOQ items.
  3. SiteLocation, access, terrain, water and safety constraints.
  4. ProgrammeExpected start date, duration and any milestone dates.

Gabion labour rate: what decides it

A gabion labour rate depends on the work, not just the headcount. We do not publish a standard labour rate on this website; it is quoted per enquiry once the scope is clear.

  • Quantity of work and unit of measurement (per box, per cubic metre or per day)
  • Stone size and filling method
  • Site access, haul distance and terrain
  • Number of workers and supervision needed
  • Duration, working season and safety requirements
  • Tools, transport and accommodation arrangement
Crew size, supervision and equipment are confirmed for each project; this website does not guarantee availability.

Enquiries from Maharashtra and the Pune region

If your project or delivery site is in Pune or elsewhere in Maharashtra, mention the exact location and PIN code in your quotation enquiry. Availability, logistics and timelines are confirmed for each site; enquiries from other states are also reviewed case by case.

Frequently asked questions

Can I request labour-only pricing?

Yes. Describe the tasks and confirm which materials, tools, transport and facilities each party provides.

What details are needed for a gabion labour quote?

Site location, work quantity or dimensions, drawings or BOQ if available, access, expected start and duration.

Do you supply labour outside the Pune area?

Project locations across Maharashtra and other states can be enquired about. Availability is confirmed per site.

Is gabion labour supply the same as gabion wall construction?

Labour supply is the work team only. Gabion wall construction describes a fuller scope that can include materials and coordination.
