Who we are

KANIFNATH CONSTRUCTION handles gabion product supply, gabion wall construction and installation, labour supply, and civil construction work such as stone pitching, UCR stone wall, soling, PCC and trimix flooring.

How we work

  • We ask for the drawing, BOQ or specification first, then discuss price.
  • We separate product supply, installation labour and construction scope in every quotation.
  • We state inclusions and exclusions - stone, geotextile, equipment, transport - in writing.
  • We confirm site feasibility and availability before committing to a schedule.

What we work on

Products: gabion boxes, GI gabion boxes, PVC-coated gabion boxes and gabion mattresses. Services: gabion wall construction, stone pitching, UCR stone wall, soling, PCC, trimix flooring and labour supply.
