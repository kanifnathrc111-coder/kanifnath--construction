Privacy Policy

How this website handles your information.

1. Scope

This policy describes the website of KANIFNATH CONSTRUCTION. Last updated 17 October 2026.

2. Enquiry forms

The enquiry forms on this website do not send your information to our server and do not store it in any database. The form prepares a message in your browser. You choose whether to open WhatsApp or your email application with that message, or to copy it. Information is shared with us only when you send the message yourself.

3. Contacting us

When you use WhatsApp, a phone call or email, you are using that service under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Cookies and analytics

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics by default. If analytics is added in future, this policy will be updated first and consent will be requested where required.

5. External content

The website loads its images, fonts and scripts from its own files. Links to external websites, such as WhatsApp, are governed by those sites.

6. Photographs

Photographs in the gallery show our own work. Where a client or site must remain unnamed, we do not name it.

7. Your requests

Because the website does not store enquiry data, there is nothing on the website to retrieve or delete. If you have sent us a message by WhatsApp, phone or email and want it removed from our records, contact us using the details on the contact page.
