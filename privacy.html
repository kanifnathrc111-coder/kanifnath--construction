Privacy Policy

How this website currently handles enquiries, external contact channels and optional analytics.

1. Scope of this policy

This policy describes the current static website for KANIFNATH CONSTRUCTION. The business owner should review and update it before enabling any third-party analytics, server-side lead forms, advertising tools or customer databases.

2. Enquiry forms

The website does not currently store form submissions on its own server or in a website database. The quotation form prepares a structured enquiry message. If the verified WhatsApp number is configured, the form opens WhatsApp with the message for the visitor to review and send manually. If WhatsApp is not configured, the site displays the message for copying and states that it has not been sent. Information typed into the form remains in the visitor's browser until the visitor closes or clears the page.

3. Contacting the business

When a visitor chooses WhatsApp, telephone or email, they will be using the selected service and its own privacy terms. The website should not be described as transmitting details to the business until the visitor sends the message through the chosen contact service.

4. Cookies and analytics

No analytics measurement ID is supplied by default. If Google Analytics or another analytics tool is added, review applicable consent, cookie, retention and disclosure requirements before activation, and update this policy accordingly. Do not send personal contact details or message contents as analytics event parameters.

5. External links and images

The site may link to external websites or load illustrative photographs from external hosts. Those services may receive technical information such as the visitor's IP address and browser details. Review their policies when deciding whether to keep a remote asset or replace it with a locally hosted asset.

6. Data requests

Because no server-side enquiry database is part of this build, the site cannot retrieve or delete a form record that it has not stored. If the business later introduces a CRM, hosted form provider or database, identify the responsible contact, retention period, lawful basis and request process here.

7. Policy updates

This policy should be updated when contact channels, hosting, analytics, form processing or business practices change. Add the effective date and public privacy contact before production launch.

Owner review required: This is a practical starter policy, not legal advice. Confirm your actual data practices and obtain appropriate legal guidance where needed.
