Gabion Mattress / Reno Mattress

Gabion mattresses are lower-profile stone-filled wire-mesh units commonly specified for surface protection and channel or bank applications. The correct unit depth, mesh and coating depend on the design.

Request a Quote Contact the Team

Typical applications

Depending on the engineering design, mattress units may be used for channel lining, riverbank protection, toe protection and erosion-control works. Hydraulic conditions, foundation preparation, filters, connections and transitions are important to performance.

Gabion mattress specification checklist

Gabion mattress units are lower-profile than common gabion boxes, but their exact construction still follows the approved design. Confirm the following fields for the selected product and site.

ParameterWhat must be confirmed
Unit dimensionsLength × width × depth and quantity per unit.
Mesh construction/openingSpecified mesh type and aperture in the BOQ / technical sheet.
Wire & coatingWire diameter(s), base coating and any additional coating required.
Compartments & connectionsDiaphragm layout, lacing/connecting wire and edge details.
Stone and filter layerStone gradation, geotextile/filter requirement, foundation preparation and connections per design.

Stone size, wire diameters and coating are project-specific; do not assume a generic value is suitable for every mattress application.
