# Business details requiring owner confirmation

The provided project brief contained the business name and service goals but did not include a verified current set of public contacts, address, product technical data, project gallery or live domain status. The website deliberately avoids inventing these details.

Before launch, confirm:

1. **Business identity:** exact public business name / legal entity wording and approved logo.
2. **Public contacts:** WhatsApp number, business phone and public enquiry email.
3. **Operating base:** actual town/city/state and whether customers visit that location.
4. **Service reach:** areas where products can be delivered and construction teams are available; clarify that a nationwide website does not automatically mean nationwide delivery.
5. **Product availability:** whether the business supplies PVC-coated, GI or other gabion options; which mattress products are available.
6. **Technical specifications:** approved mesh type/opening, wire diameter, coating, dimensions, applicable standards and documentation that can be truthfully offered.
7. **Pricing:** current supply rates, labour rates, tax treatment, quotation validity and payment terms. Older rates can conflict; do not publish any rate until approved.
8. **Scope:** whether stones, geotextile, excavation, base preparation, equipment, transport, unloading, labour, design and supervision are included in each service.
9. **Delivery:** minimum order quantities, packing, freight, unloading constraints and typical lead times (only if confirmed).
10. **Projects:** genuine photos, project description, timeframe and permission to publish project/client details.
11. **Credibility claims:** registrations, certifications, insurance, approvals, client names or prior project counts only where documentary evidence exists.
12. **Domain ownership:** confirm `kanifnathconstruction.com` is registered and the owner controls DNS/hosting.
13. **Privacy and analytics:** review privacy wording before enabling analytics or a hosted form backend.

Edit `assets/js/config.js` for the public contact fields. Do not enter private keys, passwords or other credentials into frontend code.
