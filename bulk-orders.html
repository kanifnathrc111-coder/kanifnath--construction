Bulk Gabion Box Supply Enquiries

For a bulk requirement, a clear schedule helps both sides confirm specification, quantity, packing and transport assumptions before a commercial offer is prepared.

Request a Quote Contact the Team

Prepare a clean quantity schedule

Share the BOQ or list every size and quantity on its own line. Include mesh, wire and coating details for each item where they differ. Identify any approved vendor list, sample approval or documentation requirement before an order is considered final.

Plan transport and unloading early

Delivery cost and feasibility depend on the delivery point, road access, load size, vehicle restrictions, unloading facilities and timing. Tell us whether the site has a forklift, crane, labour or other unloading arrangement and whether delivery should be phased.

Confirm the commercial scope

Clarify product specifications, accessories, packing, freight, taxes, loading/unloading, inspection, lead time, payment terms, validity and any damage or shortage reporting process in the written offer or purchase order. Do not rely on a price without its stated inclusions.

For contractors and procurement teams

Attach your enquiry / RFQ document, BOQ, delivery schedule and technical specification. Include the contact person and a preferred way to clarify details. We will review the details and identify any gaps that need confirmation.

Quotation checklist

  • RFQ / BOQ and approved specifications
  • Size-wise quantity schedule
  • Delivery site, unloading and access details
  • Target delivery window and purchase-order requirements
Need a project-specific answer? Share your drawings or BOQ, the required quantity, specifications and site location. General web information does not replace engineering design or the project technical specification.
