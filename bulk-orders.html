Bulk Gabion Box Orders

For large projects, a clear quantity schedule and delivery plan saves time. Prepare the details below and send them with your bulk quotation request.

Request a Quote Contact the TeamWhatsApp Enquiry

What to prepare for a bulk quotation

  • A size-wise schedule: dimensions and quantity of every unit type
  • The full technical specification: mesh, wire, coating and accessories
  • Delivery location(s), PIN codes and the required delivery window
  • Whether delivery will be in one lot or phased
  • Unloading facilities at site and any access limits for large vehicles
  • Any inspection, testing or documentation requirements
  • Your preferred payment terms and the required quotation validity

Planning delivery

Freight depends on the quantity, packing and destination. Minimum order quantities, packing method and delivery time are confirmed in the quotation for your project; this website does not state them in advance.

Multiple items in one enquiry

If you need boxes, mattresses and installation together, list each item separately and state which are supply-only. This helps compare quotations fairly.

Supply with labour

For large installation programmes, you can also enquire about gabion labour supply at the same time.

Need a project-specific answer? Share your drawings or BOQ, the required quantity, specifications and site location. General website information does not replace the engineering design or the project technical specification.

Enquiries from Maharashtra and the Pune region

If your project or delivery site is in Pune or elsewhere in Maharashtra, mention the exact location and PIN code in your quotation enquiry. Availability, logistics and timelines are confirmed for each site; enquiries from other states are also reviewed case by case.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a minimum order quantity?

Any minimum is confirmed in the written quotation for the specific product and destination.

Can deliveries be phased?

State your phasing needs in the enquiry; feasibility is confirmed for each project.

Can you quote for multiple sites?

Yes, list each site with its quantities and PIN code.

Next step
