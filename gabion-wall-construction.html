What gabion wall construction involves

Gabion wall work covers preparing the foundation, assembling and placing the baskets, filling them with stone, lacing them together and closing the lids. Drainage, backfill and geotextile are part of the structure design and should be defined before work starts.

Work process

  1. Review drawings and siteWall length, height, batter or steps, foundation level, access and working space.
  2. Prepare the baseExcavation, levelling and compaction as per the design.
  3. Assemble and place basketsUnfold, shape, wire together and position the units to the layout.
  4. Fill with stonePlace stone to the specified size and density, layer by layer, with bracing where needed.
  5. Lace, close and finishJoin units, close lids and complete backfill and drainage details as specified.

Gabion installation contractor scope

If you already have the boxes and stone and need only the work done, ask for installation or labour scope - see gabion labour supply. If you need supply and installation together, mention both in your enquiry.

Information we need

  • Drawings or BOQ with wall length, height and cross-section
  • Foundation and ground conditions
  • Who supplies gabion units, stone, geotextile and equipment
  • Access for vehicles and materials, and any working restrictions
  • Expected start date and duration

What affects the cost

  • Quantity of work (wall volume or number of units)
  • Stone size, source and haul distance
  • Site access, terrain and water
  • Foundation and excavation work
  • Equipment and labour requirement
  • Duration and site conditions
Design responsibility: engineering design, stability checks, drawings and approvals are not included unless explicitly agreed in writing.

Is stone included in the quotation?

That depends on the agreed scope. The written quotation states who supplies stone, transport and equipment.

Can I get the gabion boxes and the wall work together?

Yes. Select 'supply with installation' in the quote form and list both.
