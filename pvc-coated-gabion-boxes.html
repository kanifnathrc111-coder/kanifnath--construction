What is a PVC-coated gabion box?

A PVC-coated gabion box is built from steel wire that is first zinc-coated and then covered with a PVC layer. The added layer is intended to give extra protection in environments where bare zinc-coated wire may be a concern, for example where the water, soil or atmosphere is more aggressive. Whether PVC coating is justified is a design decision.

Where PVC-coated gabions are considered

  • Sites with aggressive water, soil or atmospheric conditions, as identified by the designer
  • Projects whose specification calls for a coated mesh
  • Long-life structures where the client or consultant requires an additional protective layer

Frequently asked questions

When is PVC-coated mesh required?

When the project specification or designer requires it, usually because of site conditions. We cannot decide this without the design details.

Is PVC-coated mesh more expensive?

It generally costs more than uncoated zinc-coated mesh because of the additional coating. The actual difference is shown in a written quotation.

Can I get PVC-coated boxes with installation?

State 'supply with installation' in your enquiry. Installation scope is described under gabion wall construction.
