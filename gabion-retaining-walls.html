Gabion Retaining Walls

Gabion retaining walls can provide a permeable, stone-faced retaining system when designed for the ground, loads and drainage conditions at the site.

Request a Quote Contact the Team

Where retaining gabions may be used

Project applications may include road embankments, land level changes, erosion-prone banks and selected infrastructure or landscaping works. Suitability depends on wall height, soil properties, groundwater, nearby loads, available footprint and other engineering factors.

Important design and construction considerations

Review foundation bearing, overall and internal stability, sliding and overturning, drainage, filter compatibility, backfill quality, stone grading, wire durability and construction access. These checks are site-specific and should not be replaced by a generic web diagram.

How we scope a retaining-wall enquiry

Share the location, approximate wall length and height, a section or design drawing, soil/ground information if available, expected retained loads, BOQ and whether the scope includes design, material supply or installation. Engineering design responsibilities should be explicitly agreed.

Quotation checklist

  • Drawings and wall cross-section
  • Wall height profile and length
  • Ground, water and adjacent-load context
  • Defined design, supply and installation responsibilities
Need a project-specific answer? Share your drawings or BOQ, the required quantity, specifications and site location. General web information does not replace engineering design or the project technical specification.
