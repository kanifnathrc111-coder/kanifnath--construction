UCR stone wall construction

UCR stone masonry for retaining, foundation and boundary walls.

Uncoursed rubble (UCR) masonry fits irregular local stone into cement-mortar walls. It is a robust choice where stone is available nearby and a plain, strong wall is needed.

  • Retaining walls beside plots, roads and slopes, with weep holes and back drainage as designed
  • Foundation, plinth, compound and boundary walls
  • Quotation based on your drawing, wall dimensions and who supplies stone, cement and sand

What do I need to send for a gabion box quotation?

The drawing or BOQ if you have one, the size-wise quantity, the coating you want (GI or PVC-coated), and the delivery PIN code. If something is not final, share what you have and say so.

Do you do gabion wall construction as well as supply?

Yes. Product supply, labour and construction scope are kept separate in each quotation, so you can see exactly what is included.

What information is needed for a UCR stone wall quote?

Wall length, height and thickness, the foundation depth, and who will supply stone, cement and sand. A drawing or site photograph helps.

Can I send my requirement on WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp button on this site, or call either number listed in the header. Drawings and site photographs can be shared there.

Planning a gabion or civil construction project?

Share the details you already have. For larger requirements, prepare a size-wise schedule for a bulk order. We will make the next steps clear and flag any information still needed.
