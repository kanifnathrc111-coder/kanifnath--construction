Labour for civil work

Labour supply can support work such as stone and masonry work, concrete and PCC, soling, flooring and general site support. The trades and numbers available are confirmed for each enquiry.

Work types you can enquire about

  • Gabion work - see gabion labour supply
  • Stone pitching and UCR stone wall work
  • Soling, PCC and flooring support
  • General site labour and material handling
  • Brickwork and plaster support (enquire to confirm availability)

What to tell us

  1. Trade and skill levelWhich tasks and whether skilled or general labour.
  2. Number of workersHeadcount and whether it changes over time.
  3. Duration and shiftsStart date, duration and working hours.
  4. Site and responsibilitiesLocation, access, accommodation, tools, supervision and safety.

What affects labour pricing

  • Trade, skill level and headcount
  • Duration and working hours
  • Location and travel
  • Accommodation and facilities
  • Tools, supervision and safety arrangements
  • Statutory and commercial terms

Enquiries from Maharashtra and the Pune region

If your project or delivery site is in Pune or elsewhere in Maharashtra, mention the exact location and PIN code in your quotation enquiry. Availability, logistics and timelines are confirmed for each site; enquiries from other states are also reviewed case by case.
