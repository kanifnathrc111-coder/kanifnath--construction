What is stone pitching?

Stone pitching is the placing of stone on a prepared slope to protect it from erosion by flowing water, wave action or rainfall. It may be laid dry or set in mortar, and often sits on a bedding or filter layer with a toe or edge support. The type is chosen by the designer.

Where it is used

  • Canal and drain side slopes
  • Riverbank and stream-bank protection
  • Embankments around bridges and culverts
  • Road and reservoir embankments

Information to share

  • Slope length, height and total area
  • Dry or mortar-set pitching, thickness and stone size
  • Bedding or filter layer details
  • Source of stone and who supplies it
  • Site access and working conditions

Work process

  1. Prepare the slopeTrim and compact to the required profile.
  2. Lay the bedding or filter layerAs per the design.
  3. Place the stoneDress and fit stone to the specified thickness and pattern.
  4. Complete toe and edge detailsFinish as per drawings.

What affects the rate

  • Area and thickness of pitching
  • Dry or mortar-set method
  • Stone supply and haul distance
  • Slope steepness and access
  • Water conditions during work
