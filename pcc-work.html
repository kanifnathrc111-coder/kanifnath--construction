What is PCC?

PCC is plain, unreinforced concrete placed as a clean, level base below footings, floors and other structures. It provides a firm working surface and separates the structure from the soil.

Information to share

  • Concrete grade or mix as per drawings
  • Thickness and total area or volume
  • Sub-base: soling, compacted soil or other
  • Concrete supply: site mix or ready-mix, and who arranges it
  • Formwork, curing and finish requirements
  • Access, pumping or placing arrangements

Work process

  1. Check the baseLevel and condition of soling or sub-grade.
  2. Set formwork and levelsFix edges and level pegs.
  3. Place and compact concretePlace, spread and compact to level.
  4. CureCuring as specified.
