Plain Cement Concrete (PCC) Work

PCC work should be priced to the approved mix or grade, dimensions, preparation and finishing requirements. The quotation should distinguish material, labour, equipment and measurement assumptions.

Request a Quote Contact the Team

Specify concrete requirements

Share grade or mix details as prescribed by the project, placement thickness, width and length, sub-base preparation, edge/formwork requirements, level and finish, joints, placement access and curing requirements.

Confirm inclusions and exclusions

The price may vary depending on whether the scope includes cement, aggregates, sand, water, batching, transport, pumping or placing equipment, formwork, levelling, finishing, curing, testing, labour and disposal. List the measurement unit and wastage assumptions in writing.

Request a PCC quotation

Attach the BOQ or drawing and give the site location, estimated quantity, pour access and expected schedule. Mix selection and structural requirements should be confirmed by the responsible project engineer.

Quotation checklist

  • Grade / mix specification
  • Dimensions, levels and thickness
  • Preparation, formwork and finishing
  • Material, equipment, testing and curing scope
