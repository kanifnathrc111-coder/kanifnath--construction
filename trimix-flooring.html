What is trimix flooring?

Trimix flooring is a concrete floor laid and finished to give a level, hard-wearing surface, usually for industrial and warehouse use. The exact method - mix design, surface finish, joints and curing - follows the project specification.

Information to share

  • Floor area and thickness
  • Expected loads and traffic (for example forklifts or racking)
  • Concrete grade and surface finish required
  • Joint layout and any reinforcement
  • Sub-base: soling, PCC, compacted base
  • Access, time window and curing arrangement

Work process

  1. Check the sub-baseSoling, PCC and levels.
  2. Set levels and jointsFix screed rails and joint layout.
  3. Place and finish concretePlace, level and finish to the specified surface.
  4. Cut joints and cureJoint cutting and curing as specified.

What affects the rate

  • Area and thickness
  • Concrete grade and finish
  • Reinforcement and joints
  • Access and schedule
  • Material supply arrangement
