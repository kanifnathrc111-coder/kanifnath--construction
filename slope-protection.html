Slope Protection & Erosion Control

Slope protection starts with understanding why the slope is eroding and how surface water, groundwater, soil type and site geometry affect the problem. The appropriate treatment is project-specific.

Request a Quote Contact the Team

Start with site conditions

Runoff direction, concentrated discharge, seepage, soil erosion, slope height, toe condition, access and nearby infrastructure all influence the solution. Gabions or stone pitching may be parts of a broader design rather than a complete answer on their own.

Possible work elements

Depending on the approved design, scope may include surface preparation, gabion mattress or wall installation, stone pitching, toe protection, drainage details, filter/geotextile, backfill and associated civil work. Each inclusion should be listed in the quotation.

What to share

Send the site location, photos, slope dimensions, available survey or drawings, observed erosion issue, drainage context, BOQ and whether design services are already provided. Urgent or unstable slopes require assessment by qualified professionals; a web enquiry is not a safety assessment.

Quotation checklist

  • Slope height, angle and approximate area
  • Water flow, seepage and drainage observations
  • Available engineering drawings and BOQ
  • Access, equipment and material-supply scope
Need a project-specific answer? Share your drawings or BOQ, the required quantity, specifications and site location. General web information does not replace engineering design or the project technical specification.
