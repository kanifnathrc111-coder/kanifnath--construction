What is soling?

Soling is a layer of stone or brick placed over the prepared, compacted ground and packed tightly, with the gaps filled with fine material. It spreads loads and gives a firm base for PCC, flooring or pavements. Thickness and material are set by the specification.

Information to share

  • Area in square metres and layer thickness
  • Stone or brick type and size
  • Sub-grade condition and compaction requirement
  • Material supply and haulage arrangement
  • Site access and working space

Work process

  1. Prepare the sub-gradeLevel and compact as specified.
  2. Place and pack the solingLay stones tightly in position.
  3. Fill and consolidateFill gaps with fine material and compact.
  4. Check levelsVerify finished level before PCC or flooring.

Soling is commonly followed by PCC work and then trimix flooring or other finishes.
