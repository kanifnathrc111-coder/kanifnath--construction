Soling Work

Soling work is generally specified as a prepared stone layer used as part of a project’s base or foundation build-up. Thickness, stone size, laying pattern and compaction must follow the project requirement.

Request a Quote Contact the Team

What needs to be defined

Confirm the location and area, required thickness, stone grading or type, base preparation, edge restraints, filling of voids if specified, compaction method and the layer that follows soling.

Quotation inclusions

Clarify whether excavation, disposal, stone supply, loading, transport, spreading, levelling, water, compaction equipment and labour are included. Share the BOQ and measurement unit so rates can be compared accurately.

Plan for access and logistics

Stone delivery and equipment access can affect the work sequence. Give the delivery point, unloading conditions, work area access, site restrictions and target schedule when requesting a quotation.

Quotation checklist

  • Area and layer thickness
  • Stone quality / size and compaction detail
  • Subgrade preparation and disposal needs
  • BOQ and site access arrangements
Need a project-specific answer? Share your drawings or BOQ, the required quantity, specifications and site location. General web information does not replace engineering design or the project technical specification.
