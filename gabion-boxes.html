What is a gabion box?

A gabion box is a rectangular wire-mesh container that is assembled on site and filled with suitable stone. Filled units are stacked or placed to form gravity retaining structures, channel and riverbank protection, erosion-control works and landscape walls. The mesh holds the stone in place while the structure stays free-draining and able to tolerate some movement.

Gabion box types we can quote

  • GI gabion boxes - zinc-coated (galvanised) wire mesh for general civil applications.
  • PVC-coated gabion boxes - PVC coating over galvanised wire where a more demanding environment is expected.
  • Gabion (Reno) mattresses - shallow, wide units used for channel beds, bank lining and slope surfaces.

Which product suits your site depends on the project design, the environment and the specification issued by the project authority or consultant. We do not recommend a product without knowing those details.

Where gabion boxes are used

  • Retaining walls and road-side or hill-side embankment support
  • Riverbank, stream and canal bank protection
  • Erosion control on slopes and around culverts or bridge abutments
  • Landscape walls, boundary structures and terracing
