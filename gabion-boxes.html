What is a gabion box?

A gabion box is a wire-mesh container assembled and filled with suitable stone. Depending on the project design, gabions can form gravity retaining structures, channel or riverbank protection, erosion-control features and landscape elements. Product selection should match the engineering design and site conditions.

Supply-only or supply with installation

A supply-only request typically covers the agreed gabion units and listed accessories. Installation work can involve basket assembly, positioning, bracing, stone filling, lacing and site-specific preparation. Confirm whether stone, geotextile, excavation, foundation, equipment, labour and transport are included or excluded.
